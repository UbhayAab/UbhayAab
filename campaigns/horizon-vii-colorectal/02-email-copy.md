## P1 · Human reply playbook — by intent

The person staffing the inbox tags every reply with one of six intents and uses the matching opener. **Never paste these verbatim** — they're openers, and the specific answer to what they actually wrote goes underneath.

### Intent 1 — "Yes, I'm interested" / "Please register me"
**Action:** register them manually, then confirm. Move to Track R.
```
Dr. {{last_name}} — done, you're registered. Your join link:
{{zoom_join_link}}

I've also attached a calendar invite. If you have a question you'd like put to
the panel, send it to me and I'll get it into the 8:45 PM Q&A.

Looking forward to having you there on the 26th.
```

### Intent 2 — A clinical or programme question
**Action:** answer properly, then register them. If it needs faculty input, say so and come back with the answer — don't guess.
```
Dr. {{last_name}} — good question.

{{ACTUAL ANSWER — specific, and if it needs faculty input, say
"I'm checking with Dr. {{X}} and will come back to you by {{day}}."}}

Since it's directly relevant to the 26th, would you like me to put this to the
panel during the Q&A? It goes to the faculty anonymously.

And I can register you now if it's useful — just say the word.
```

### Intent 3 — "Can't attend / wrong timezone / clashing with duty"
**Action:** flip to the recording opt-in list. This is a conversion, not a loss.
```
Completely understood — Saturday evening is a real ask.

Register anyway and I'll make sure the recording reaches you Sunday morning;
it goes to registrants only. {{registration_url}}

If it's a timezone issue: we're at 7:00 PM IST, which is 9:30 AM Houston and
2:30 PM London. If neither works, the recording is the answer.
```

### Intent 4 — "I'd like to present / speak / partner / sponsor"
**Action:** exit automation, route to the programme team same day. This is how Edition VIII gets built.
```
Thank you — genuinely glad you wrote.

I'm passing this to {{programme_lead}}, who runs faculty and partnerships for
Horizon, and they'll come back to you within two working days.

Would you be open to a short call next week? If so, send a couple of windows
that suit you.
```

### Intent 5 — A patient or caregiver reply
**Action:** warm, human, and clear about limits. Never give individual medical advice from the campaign inbox.
```
Dear {{first_name}},

Thank you for writing.

Horizon is a session for clinicians, and you're welcome to listen — Priyanka
Joshi from Jarurat Care speaks at 7:05 PM on what the cancer care journey
looks like from the patient's side. Much of the rest of the evening is
technical.

I'm not able to advise on your own treatment from here — that has to come from
your treating team, who know your case. What I can do is point you to Jarurat
Care's patient support services: {{patient_support_link}}

Wishing you and your family strength.
```

### Intent 6 — Annoyed / "stop emailing me" / any negative signal
**Action:** suppress immediately, apologise once, briefly, and stop. Do not attempt to save it.
```
Done — you've been removed from the Horizon list and won't hear from us again.

Apologies for the intrusion, and thank you for telling us.
```

**Escalation:** anything involving a complaint, a legal or regulatory question, a media enquiry, or an adverse-event mention goes to {{escalation_owner}} the same day and out of the marketing inbox entirely.
